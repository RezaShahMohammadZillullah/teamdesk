Quick Start Guide

Start a support session in three simple steps

Designed for engineering clients, project partners, and internal teammates to collaborate safely across locations without complicated installation steps.

01

Open App or Install

Launch Vartex TeamDesk in your browser at teamdesk.vartexengg.com or install the packaged Windows app from the Microsoft Store.

02

Share Your Peer ID

Copy the temporary 9-digit Peer ID shown in your session panel and share it directly with your Vartex Engineering specialist or teammate.

03

Approve & Collaborate

Confirm screen sharing and remote control when prompted. Pause remote input or end the session anytime with one click from the system tray.

No Silent Background Control Remote mouse and keyboard access activates only after explicit host initiation.
End-to-End Encrypted Transport Signaling, screen video, and session input travel over TLS/HTTPS, WSS, and WebRTC.
Instant Pause & Exit Keep full ownership of your desktop with visible Windows system tray controls.
